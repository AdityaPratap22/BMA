## The Death of Last-Click Attribution

For over a decade, marketing teams relied on browser cookie tracking and Google Analytics last-click models. Today, privacy frameworks (iOS App Tracking Transparency, third-party cookie deprecation) have made simple browser analytics fundamentally inaccurate.

If you judge campaign viability strictly on platform-reported ROAS, you will inevitably underinvest in high-impact top-of-funnel channels and overspend on branded search clicks that would have converted organically.

```
Attribution Reality:
User Sees Meta Reel (Day 1) ──► Reads LinkedIn Article (Day 4) ──► Searches Brand on Google (Day 9) ──► Converts (Google gets 100% credit)
```

## Core Financial Metrics Every CMO Must Monitor

### 1. Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER)
$$\text{MER} = \frac{\text{Total Revenue Generated}}{\text{Total Blended Marketing Spend}}$$
MER provides the true north-star metric. When MER is above 4.0x, aggressive budget scaling is financially justified regardless of individual channel reporting discrepancy.

### 2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) to Lifetime Value (LTV)
$$\text{Target LTV:CAC Ratio} \ge 3.0x$$
Track cohort LTV at 30, 60, 90, and 365-day intervals to calculate the exact payback period of paid marketing capital.

## Technical Measurement Infrastructure Checklist

1. **Server-Side Tracking (CAPI)**: Implement Meta Conversions API and Google Tag Manager Server-Side container to recover 25-35% of lost browser events.
2. **UTM Governance Framework**: Standardize strict taxonomy across all campaigns: `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`, and `utm_term`.
3. **Post-Purchase Attribution Surveys**: Add a 1-question zero-party data prompt: *"How did you first hear about us?"*.
